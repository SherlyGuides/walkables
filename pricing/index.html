Questions

The reasoning.

Why a monthly plan and not a price per link?

Storage and bandwidth cost scale with time and views, not with the number of links. A monthly plan with unlimited links means you never hesitate to send one.

Why do links expire at all?

Expiry caps what is stored for a client who stopped looking months ago. On Studio a link lives 30 days and renews in one click, because a client review rarely fits in a week and re-uploading mid-project is the thing that drives people away.

What happens to my links if I stop paying?

They move to the Free limits at the end of the billing month: one live link, 7 days, the small tag. Your scenes and settings stay; nothing is deleted.

Does my client need an account?

No. A link, and a PIN if you set one. Nothing to install on either side.

Are prices in rupees?

Yes. Taxes as applicable at the time of billing.
