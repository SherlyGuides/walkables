Export guide

Getting a GLB out of your tool.

walkable reads GLB files. Find your tool below. If it is not listed, export FBX or OBJ, open it in Blender (free) and follow the Blender steps.

BlenderFile › Export › glTF 2.0. Format glTF Binary (.glb), keep +Y Up, tick Apply Modifiers. Delete cameras and lights first.
Rhino 7 and 8File › Export Selected › glTF. Choose Binary (.glb), tick Draco compression, keep textures embedded. Use a moderate mesh density.
3ds Max2023 and later: File › Export › glTF. Earlier: the free Babylon.js exporter, output glb. Convert materials to Physical Material first.
RevitInstall a glTF exporter add-in from the Autodesk App Store and export .glb from a 3D view. Or export FBX and convert it in Blender.
SketchUpInstall a glTF extension from the Extension Warehouse. Or export COLLADA (.dae) with textures and convert it in Blender.
ArchiCADFrom the 3D window, Save As COLLADA or OBJ, then convert it in Blender.

Before you export

  • Send one floor, not the site. Hide terrain, context buildings and planting.
  • Open the doors your client should walk through. A closed door is a wall.
  • Export the floor. Without one, your client falls through.
  • Swap heavy furniture downloaded from manufacturers for simple versions.
  • Keep textures at 2K or smaller.

Aim for under 10 MB and under 700,000 triangles. The try page shows both numbers the moment you drop a file, and warns when a model is too heavy for a phone.
